About Medicall

A smarter way to connect
hospitals with patients.

Medicall is an AI-powered receptionist designed specifically for healthcare. It helps hospitals handle patient calls, answer common questions, assist with appointment-related requests, and make sure patients reach the right place at the right time.

Designed to reduce the communication workload on hospital reception teams, Medicall makes the patient experience faster and more convenient — without replacing the human touch that healthcare demands.

How It Works

From patient call to the right care —
automatically.

Four simple steps from the patient picking up the phone to reaching the right care.

01

Patient Calls

A patient calls the hospital — Medicall picks up instantly, every time, day or night.

02

Medicall Answers

The AI receptionist greets the patient, understands their request, and responds naturally in real time.

03

Medicall Assists

It answers questions, helps with appointments, collects information, and guides the patient through their request.

04

Connect When Needed

Complex or important requests are directed to the right hospital team, ensuring no patient is left without care.

Appointment Assistance
